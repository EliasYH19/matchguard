How it works

Three steps, from purchase to a clean bracket.

1. Organizer buys a package

Pick Bronze, Silver or Gold based on your player count and how deep you want the scan to go, then create your tournament in a couple of minutes.

2. Players register & submit a scan

Each player registers with their gamertag and uploads a system/process log. MatchGuard hashes the file and checks it against our cheat-signature list.

3. You get a live flagged list

The organizer dashboard shows every scan result in real time, and clean players receive a signed verification certificate automatically.

Built for both formats

Doesn't matter if your bracket is played from bedrooms or on folding tables.

Online tournaments

Players upload their scan from home before check-in closes. Works great for weekly ladders and regional online cups run over Discord/matchmaking lobbies.

LAN-hosted tournaments

Gold-tier kiosk mode lets you run the scan upload station right at the door - no player sits down at their PC until their check comes back clear.
